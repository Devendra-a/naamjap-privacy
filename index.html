Privacy Policy

App: Naam Jap (package com.padhayikro.naamjap) · Developer: Padhayikro · Effective date: 14 October 2026

In short. Naam Jap is a mantra / jap counter. Your counts, history, mantras and settings stay on your phone. We have no accounts, no servers that receive your data, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of our own. The app shows ads from Google AdMob and sells an optional yearly Premium subscription through Google Play; those two Google services process some technical data as described below.

संक्षेप में। नाम जप एक मंत्र/जप काउंटर है। आपकी गिनती, इतिहास, मंत्र और सेटिंग्स आपके फ़ोन में ही रहती हैं। हमारा कोई अकाउंट-सिस्टम या सर्वर नहीं है जो आपका डेटा लेता हो। ऐप में Google AdMob के विज्ञापन दिखते हैं और Google Play के ज़रिये वैकल्पिक सालाना Premium (विज्ञापन-मुक्त) सदस्यता मिलती है; इन दोनों Google सेवाओं द्वारा कुछ तकनीकी जानकारी प्रोसेस होती है (नीचे विस्तार से)।

1. Information stored on your device

The following is created by you while using the app and is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see it.

  • Jap counts, daily totals, history entries (date, time, mantra, count, duration, how the count was made) and streaks.
  • Your mantras (names you add or edit), targets and mala size.
  • Settings such as theme, sound and vibration, reminder times, floating-bubble size and opacity, counting mode and your Premium status.
  • If you choose a photo as the counter background, a copy of that one photo inside the app's storage.

Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Data → Reset all data, deletes this information from your phone.

2. Permissions and why the app asks for them

The app does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, SMS, call logs or files. When you pick a background photo or an import file, Android's own picker gives the app only the single item you selected.

3. Microphone and voice counting

  • Sound pulse mode: the microphone level is analysed live on the phone to notice each chant. Audio is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere; it is discarded immediately.
  • Speech recognition mode: uses the speech-recognition service built into your Android phone (normally provided by Google). Audio may be sent to that service to be turned into text, under the privacy policy of its provider. Naam Jap only receives the recognised words to count your mantra and does not store them or the audio. You can use Sound pulse mode instead.
  • The microphone is used only while Voice mode is running on screen, and you can revoke the permission any time in Android settings.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad supplied by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system information, ad interactions and app-usage information related to ads. Naam Jap does not give Google any of your jap data.

  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before ads are requested. You can change the choice later where the app offers it, and always in your Android settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Subscribing to Premium removes ads.
  • More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional yearly Premium subscription is bought and managed through Google Play. Payment details are handled entirely by Google; Naam Jap never sees or stores your card, UPI or bank details. The app only learns whether your subscription is active and keeps that status on your phone. You can cancel or manage the subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

6. Donations

If a donation or "support the developer" option is offered in the app or on the app's store page, it is voluntary, does not unlock any feature and is processed by the payment provider shown at checkout (for example Google Play or a UPI / payment provider). Naam Jap does not receive or store your card, UPI or bank details; the provider's own privacy policy applies to that payment. The developer may receive the donor name or contact information only if the provider shares it with the payment.

7. Backups, exports and sharing

You can export a backup or a CSV of your history as a file and share it with any app you choose (for example Drive or WhatsApp), and import a backup file later. These files are created and sent only when you ask, and what happens to them afterwards depends on the app you share them with. Naam Jap does not upload them anywhere.

8. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in, no profile.
  • No sale or rental of personal information by the developer.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash-reporting SDK of our own. (Google AdMob and Google Play Services process data as described above.)
  • No use of your jap data for advertising.

9. Children

Naam Jap is a devotional counting utility for everyone. It is not designed or marketed to children, and it does not ask anyone for a name, age or any other personal detail. Younger children may use it together with a parent or guardian. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under the minimum age set by your country); the only data processed by third parties is the advertising and purchase data described in sections 4 and 5, which is handled by Google under its own policies. Parents and guardians can turn off ads by subscribing to Premium, restrict purchases with Google Play's parental controls, and revoke any permission in Android settings. If you believe a child has used the app in a way you want to change, please contact us.

10. Data retention and your choices

  • Your jap data lives on your phone until you delete it, reset it in the app, clear the app's storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, we cannot recover it for you. Use the backup export to keep your own copy.
  • You can turn off the microphone, overlay and notification permissions at any time in Android settings; the related features then stop.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, the California CCPA/CPRA or India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. The developer holds no personal data about you; for data processed by Google (ads and billing) please use Google's own privacy controls at myaccount.google.com. You can also contact us using the details below and we will help as far as we are able.

12. Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, protected by Android's app sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Keep your phone locked and protect your backup files.

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example when a new feature or service is added). The effective date at the top shows the latest version, and material changes will be mentioned in the app's release notes.
